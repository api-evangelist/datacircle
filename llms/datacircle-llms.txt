# Datacircle

> Datacircle lists all the GTM APIs

Every GTM API we find, one row for each plan of each call: what it costs per 1,000, what it charges when it finds nothing, its rate limits, its speed and the fields it returns.

## Questions

- What is Datacircle? Datacircle lists all the GTM APIs
- Who is it for? GTM API providers, and the teams who buy from them.
- What is in the sheet? Every GTM API we find, one row for each plan of each call: what it costs per 1,000, what it charges when it finds nothing, its rate limits, its speed and the fields it returns.
- How is the sheet sorted? By Overall score, best first. A plan we haven't benchmarked has none yet, so it comes after, by provider, A to Z. On All GTM APIs and each category's sheet, we group each API's plans. Sort by any column from its header menu, and click Reset sort to return to this order.
- Why are some plans hidden at first? By default we show each provider's best plan, and each API's top 3 plans on All GTM APIs and each category's sheet, without plans bought on Apify or RapidAPI. Turn off the filters in the bar to see every plan. We don't score plans bought on Apify or RapidAPI for now: we're planning a section of their own for them. We also hide plans that need your own LinkedIn account, and don't score them: they run on your account, not the provider's.
- How do you score each plan? We score each plan on five parts and scale the total to 100. A plan's price weighs 40, its data 40, its cost to get started 10, its bundled calls 10 and its performance 10. We score data and performance from our benchmark of the provider's call. Until we benchmark it, they are N/A, and so is the plan's Overall score. A plan whose data score is 0 has an Overall score of 0, whatever its other scores. We rank its price against the other scored plans of its API. Click Score in the sheet's bar to change these weights. We give N/A to free, yearly and contact us plans, plans over $500 to start, plans bought on a marketplace and plans that need your own LinkedIn account.
- How do you price a waterfall? A waterfall tries one provider after another until one finds the answer. Its Waterfall column lists each provider in order, with its price and how often it finds one. On a paid plan, its $ per 1,000 is what a call costs on average.
- Where do the numbers come from? From each provider's own pricing page and docs, and from what providers tell us. The CSV lists the pages we read each row from.
- Does each plan have its own page? Yes. Each API plan on the sheet links to its own page, with an llms.txt for AI agents.
- Can I download the sheet? Yes. "Download CSV", above the sheet, gives every row.
- Can I get it as JSON? Yes. "Download JSON" gives every row as one array. "Download zip" gives the files we build the sheet from, one for each data source, provider and plan.
- Do I sign up to download? No. Anyone can download the sheet as CSV, JSON or a zip, with no sign-up.
- Do you have an API, an MCP server or a plugin? We closed our API and our plugins. Download every GTM API plan on Datacircle as one file, with a skill that tells your AI how to query it: datacircle.dev/SKILL.md. Our MCP server is open to anyone, with no key: add api.datacircle.dev/mcp to Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor.
- What does the MCP server do? It answers from the sheet. list_apis lists the GTM APIs on it, and search_plans finds their plans by API, provider or price. Each plan comes with its score, and the best come first, as on the sheet.
- What does each column mean? "Fields explained", above the sheet, explains each column and key.
- Do you sell leads or contact lists? No. We list GTM APIs and what they cost. We sell no data about people.
- Do you have a Chrome extension? No. Datacircle is a sheet of GTM APIs.
- An API is missing, or a row is wrong? Email wayne@datacircle.dev, and we add it or fix it. For now we don't add or update plans on Apify or RapidAPI: we're planning a separate section for them.
- How does the sheet help a provider? Buyers compare plans on the sheet. Each plan's page links to the provider's site and API docs.
- Is there a group for providers? Yes, on WhatsApp. Sign up at datacircle.dev/login with an email at your website's domain, and your dashboard links to it.
- Are you buying from us? No. We list your plans for the people who buy them.
- Can you list my other API? Yes. Send its pricing page and docs to wayne@datacircle.dev, and we add it. For now we don't add plans on Apify or RapidAPI: we're planning a separate section for them.
- Can you show my volume price? We list the price each plan's page gives. A plan sold on request is listed as contact us.
- What name do you list us under? Each plan is listed under the name its provider gives it.
- Can I pay to be listed, or to rank higher? Being on the sheet costs nothing. For anything more, we're open to it: email wayne@datacircle.dev.
